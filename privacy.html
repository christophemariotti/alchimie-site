2. In short

  • The game works without an account and offline. Your progress is stored on your phone.
  • We run no servers: we receive no data about you.
  • The free version shows Google ads (AdMob) between levels and when you choose to watch a video. On first launch you decide whether ads may be personalised; change your mind at any time in Settings → Privacy and ads.
  • A purchase (“No ads” or “Alchemist Pass”) removes all ads: the ads SDK is then no longer started.

5. What we do not do

  • No collection by us, no data sale, no profiling.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • No ads during a game, no banners.

6. Children

Alchimie is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not part of Google Play's Families programme.

7. Your rights

Your game data is on your phone: uninstall the game to erase it (or Android Settings → Apps → Alchimie → Storage → Clear data). Withdraw ad consent in Settings → Privacy and ads. For data processed by Google, contact Google (myaccount.google.com). You may also contact your data protection authority (in France, CNIL).
